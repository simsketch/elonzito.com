# Profile

## Who Elon Zito is

Elon Zito is a machine learning and platform engineering leader who takes generative-AI systems from architecture to production in regulated enterprise environments. He has more than fifteen years of experience spanning full-stack development, cloud architecture, UX design, and engineering leadership.

Most recently he led a distributed team delivering two AI platforms for a Fortune 200 utility's treasury organization: a governed system of record that replaced spreadsheet tracking of multi-billion-dollar credit portfolios, and a retrieval-augmented diligence engine built on hybrid search, source-authority re-ranking, multi-agent consensus, and calibrated LLM evaluation.

He is certified in Generative AI (Generative AI with Large Language Models, Coursera, May 2024).
